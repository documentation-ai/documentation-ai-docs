Use this checklist alongside [Migrate to Documentation.AI](/migrations/overview). Each item is short, and each links to the details where you need them.

## Before you start

- You own the site, or its owner has given you permission to copy it.
- You have the **Editor** or **Admin** role in the organization that owns your Documentation.AI project.
- Every plan includes preview sites, so you can check the result before it goes live.
- The migrator is installed. See [Install the migrator](/migrations/install).
- You know how you want to deliver: without Git, or with Git. See [Choose how to deliver](/migrations/overview#choose-how-to-deliver).
- If you migrate with Git, your project's repository is cloned on your computer and you can push to it.
- You have picked a template. See [Templates](/customize/templates).
- Your team knows to stop editing the old site during the migration, so no new change is left behind.

## At each decision

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Decision 1: pages and structure" default-open="false">

    - Every page you expect is in the list.
    - Tabs, groups, languages, versions, and their order match your old sidebar.
    - Any page you leave out has a reason.
    - You chose whether to add pages that are not in your old sidebar.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Decision 2: conversion plan" default-open="false">

    - Every component without a clear equivalent has a decision.
    - Page addresses stay the same, or each changed address has a redirect.
    - Colors, logo, favicon, top bar links, and template look right.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Decision 3: converted site" default-open="false">

    - The checks passed, or you understand each finding.
    - You opened a few converted pages, including your most visited ones.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Decision 4: preview" default-open="false">

    - Every preview page passed the check, or you accepted it with a reason.
    - Your most visited pages look right on a phone and on a desktop.
    - Search, the sidebar, and API reference pages work.
    - You read the report and shared it with anyone who needs to sign off.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Go live

- Merge the migration branch. See [Go live](/migrations/overview#go-live).
- Connect your domain. See [Custom Domain Setup](/customize/custom-domain), or [Custom Subpath Setup](/customize/custom-subpath/overview) if your docs live under a path such as `/docs`.
- Open a few old page addresses and check that they reach the right pages.

## After you go live

- Keep your old site online until your domain points to Documentation.AI.
- Update links to your documentation in your product, emails, and help articles.
- Delete the migration branch when you no longer need it. See [Branches and previews](/write-and-publish/branches-and-previews).
