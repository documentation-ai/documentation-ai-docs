## Feature details

### Paper Library

Store research PDFs in a centralized library with automatic metadata extraction, tagging, and custom collections. Organize papers by project, topic, or custom tags so your team can find related research quickly.

### Smart Search

Search the full text and metadata of every paper in your library. Search within specific collections and filter results by author, date, and tags.

<Callout kind="info">

Enterprise plans include AI-powered semantic search that finds papers by meaning, not just keywords.

</Callout>

### Citation Management

Generate citations automatically in multiple styles, including APA, MLA, Chicago, and IEEE. Export references in BibTeX or RIS format, and customize citation styles for each workspace.

### Version Tracking

Review document history, compare changes between versions, and restore an earlier version when needed. Every edit creates a version snapshot automatically.

### Collaboration

Edit papers together in real time, leave comments, and assign role-based access to control workspace permissions. See the [Collaboration page](/paper-guide/collaboration) for details on working with your research team.
