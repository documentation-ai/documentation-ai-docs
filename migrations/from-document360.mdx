You can migrate a Document360 knowledge base from an export file or from its live site. The steps are the same as for every platform. See [Migrate to Documentation.AI](/migrations/overview).

## What you can migrate from

- **An export from Document360**, as the ZIP file or its unzipped folder. It includes your articles, categories, and media. Tell your assistant where the file is.
- **Your live site**, such as `https://docs.acme.com`.

## What moves

| In Document360                                   | In Documentation.AI                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Categories and their order                       | Sidebar groups and pages, in the same order                |
| Articles from the WYSIWYG and Markdown editors   | Pages                                                      |
| Info, warning, error, and success boxes          | `Callout` with the matching style                          |
| Expandable `details` sections                    | `Expandable`                                               |
| FAQ blocks                                       | A group of expandable questions                            |
| Tabs                                             | `Tabs`                                                     |
| Tables                                           | Tables                                                     |
| Embedded frames and videos                       | Embedded frames and `Video`                                |
| Images and files in the export                   | Files in your media library                                |

## What needs your decision

- **Snippets.** An export contains snippet placeholders, such as `{{snippet.contact}}`, but not the snippet text. At the second decision, give your assistant the text for each snippet. Pages that use a snippet without its text wait until you do.
- **Workspaces and languages.** If your export mixes several, choose which to migrate at the first decision.
- **Articles outside any category.** The first decision lists them so you can place them or leave them out.

## What does not move

- Your footer, custom fonts, and banner. See [What does not move](/migrations/overview#what-does-not-move).
