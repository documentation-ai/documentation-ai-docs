Export your live site as a single PDF for offline reading, a compliance submission, a printed handover, or a customer deliverable. Pick the pages, and pick a PDF template that sets how the PDF looks: its paper, cover page, header image, page numbers, footer text, watermark, fonts, and colours. The PDF holds the pages in navigation order, with a clickable outline you can jump around in.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  **Before you begin**

  - PDF export is available on the **Professional** and **Enterprise** plans.
  - You need the **Admin** role on the documentation to start or download an export, or to change PDF templates.
  - The export reads your live site. Publish the changes you want in the PDF first. Unpublished edits and working versions are not exported.
</Callout>

## How to export your site as a PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Exports" icon="download" title-type="p">
    In the dashboard, open **Settings** and select **Exports** under **Advanced**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the export dialog" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Click **Export PDF**. If your site already has a PDF, the button reads **Export Again**, and the dialog opens on that export's file name, template, and pages.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name the PDF" icon="file-pen" title-type="p">
    **File name** is the name the PDF downloads under, with `.pdf` added for you. It starts as your site's name. Change it to say what this PDF holds, such as "Onboarding pack" for a few chosen pages. A name can be up to 100 characters and cannot contain `\ / : * ? " < > |`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a PDF template" icon="layout-template" title-type="p">
    **PDF template** shows the template the PDF prints with. It starts on your default template, tagged **Default**. Click it to pick another. To add, edit, or delete templates, see [PDF templates](#pdf-templates).

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-53a37986-2c9e-4094-b9e8-1e1ffae9e9ee/doc-b389b141-ae58-4fd5-91f9-6702fae9ac58/1795444293997-6me4u738pku-pdf-template-list-framed.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1352" height="1700" alt="The PDF template list, with Standard tagged Default, three more templates, and New template at the bottom" caption="The template list. The pencil beside a template edits it." />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the pages" icon="list-checks" title-type="p">
    An export holds every page of your site unless you narrow it. Open **Pages** to see them as they sit in your navigation, with your [products, versions, and languages](/organize/dimensions) as the top groups. Untick a page to leave it out, or untick a group to leave out every page in it. Use **Search pages** to find a page by name. The line beside **Pages** shows how many pages the PDF will hold, such as "12 of 42 pages".

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-53a37986-2c9e-4094-b9e8-1e1ffae9e9ee/doc-b389b141-ae58-4fd5-91f9-6702fae9ac58/1795444280778-idadkpdv5hn-pdf-export-dialog-framed.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1352" height="1700" alt="The export dialog with a file name, the Customer handover template, and the page list with one page unticked" caption="Untick a page or a group to leave it out of the PDF." />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the export" icon="play" title-type="p">
    Click **Export PDF**. Exports shows the progress page by page, and you can leave the page while it runs. To stop it, click **Cancel**. When it finishes, you get an email with a link back to Exports.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the PDF" icon="file-down" title-type="p">
    Click **Download PDF**. The PDF downloads under the name you gave it, which shows under the button, such as "Onboarding pack.pdf".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## PDF templates

A PDF template is a saved design for your exports. Every site starts with one template, **Standard**: A4, portrait, page numbers at the bottom centre, Inter, and your site's colours. Add your own for each kind of PDF you send out, such as a customer handover with a cover page or a draft with a watermark. A site can have up to 20 templates.

Templates live in the export dialog: you add, edit, and delete them from the **PDF template** list.

### Create or edit a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template list" icon="download" title-type="p">
    Open **Settings**, select **Exports**, click **Export PDF**, and click **PDF template** in the export dialog.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open a template" icon="pencil" title-type="p">
    Click **New template** at the bottom of the list, or the pencil beside a template to edit it. **Edit** beside the list edits the template that is picked. The template opens in place of the export dialog.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name it and set how it prints" icon="sliders-horizontal" title-type="p">
    Give the template a name your team will recognise in the export dialog, then work through the sections. Click a section to open or fold it. A section with a switch prints only while its switch is on. See [Template settings](#template-settings).

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-53a37986-2c9e-4094-b9e8-1e1ffae9e9ee/doc-b389b141-ae58-4fd5-91f9-6702fae9ac58/1795444289245-az634bsvrjq-pdf-template-editor-framed.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2344" height="2104" alt="The template editor, with its settings sections on the left and a preview of a printed page on the right" caption="The settings sections beside the preview of a page." />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make it the default, if you want" icon="star" title-type="p">
    Turn on **Default template** to have exports use this template unless another one is picked, API exports included. See [Set the default template](#set-the-default-template).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the preview" icon="eye" title-type="p">
    The preview beside the settings follows every change. When the template has a cover page, switch between **Cover** and **Page**. Use the zoom buttons to look closer, or **Fit** to see the whole sheet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save" icon="check" title-type="p">
    Click **Create template**, or **Save changes** on a template you are editing. The export dialog comes back with the template picked, and your file name and pages as you left them. **Back**, or **Export PDF** at the top, returns to the export dialog without saving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Template settings

| Section          | What you set                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Layout**       | **Page size**: A4, Letter, Legal, A3, or A5. **Orientation**: portrait or landscape. **Margins**: normal, narrow, or wide. |
| **Text styles**  | For headings: **Heading font**, **Heading size**, and **Heading colour**. For paragraphs: **Paragraph font**, **Paragraph size**, and **Paragraph colour**. Then **Link colour**. |
| **Page numbers** | "Page 1 of 10", "Page 1", or "1" at the bottom of every page, on the left, centre, or right.                                      |
| **Footer text**  | One line of up to 120 characters, such as a copyright or confidentiality notice, with its size and colour.                        |
| **Cover page**   | A first page with a **Title**, a **Description**, the export date if you want it, a **Logo** (your site's logo, another image, or none), and an **Accent colour** for the bar at the top of the cover and the line under the title. |
| **Header image** | Your site's logo or another image at the top of every page, in one of three sizes, on the left, centre, or right.                 |
| **Watermark**    | Text of up to 30 characters, or an image, printed faintly across every page. Set its angle (diagonal or straight) and opacity.    |

- **Heading size** sets how large each page's title prints, from 20 to 40 px. Smaller headings scale with it, so a page keeps its proportions. It starts at 28 px, the size titles print at on A4 paper.
- **Paragraph size** sets how large paragraphs and lists print, from 12 to 24 px. It starts at 15 px.
- **The cover's title** starts as your site's name. Change it, or leave it empty to keep printing the site's name. The title and description print in the fonts and colours of **Text styles**, at the cover's own sizes.
- **Show export date** prints the day of the export under the cover's description, such as "November 23, 2026".
- **The header image, page numbers, footer text, and watermark** print on every page after the cover. The cover counts as page 1, so the page after it reads "Page 2".
- **Footer text** prints on the line above the page numbers, on the same side. A line too long for the page is cut short.
- **Page numbers** read in English, whatever the language of the pages.
- **Images** come from your [media library](/write-and-publish/media-library). Click **Choose image** to pick one or upload a new one. A template prints PNG, JPEG, SVG, and WebP images.
- **Site logo** is the logo of your live site. On a site without a logo, the cover and the header image take an image of their own instead.
- **Fonts** are Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Montserrat, Source Sans 3, Noto Sans, IBM Plex Sans, Merriweather, Lora, Noto Serif, and Source Serif 4.
- **Colours** left empty print with your site's own colours.

### Set the default template

The default template is the one the export dialog picks first, and the one the API prints with when a request names none. It carries a **Default** tag in the template list. To change it, open another template, turn on **Default template**, and save. The current default's switch stays on: to stop a template being the default, make another one the default.

### Delete a template

In the template list, click the delete icon beside a template and confirm. A PDF already exported with it stays as it is. The default template can't be deleted, so make another template the default first.

## What the PDF contains

- The cover page, when the template has one.
- The pages you ticked, in navigation order.
- A clickable outline in your PDF reader's sidebar, built from each page's headings.
- The content of your live site at the moment the export starts.
- A page that cannot be rendered, for example because its MDX does not compile, is left out rather than failing the export.

## Things to know

- **One PDF is kept.** A new export replaces the current PDF once it finishes, so download the PDF you need before you start the next one.
- **One export at a time.** Wait for the running export to finish, or cancel it, before starting another.
- **Template changes apply to the next export.** Editing or deleting a template does not change a PDF you already exported.
- **Up to 8,000 pages.** Large sites can take several minutes to export.
- **A single page** can be saved by readers from the docs site with [Download as PDF](/ai/page-actions#download-as-pdf-pdf).

## Export with the API

To export from a script or a CI pipeline, use the REST API. It takes a template and a list of pages, as the dialog does. A PDF exported with the API downloads as `documentation-export.pdf`. See [PDF Exports](/api-reference/overview#pdf-exports).
