## Overview

The AI Assistant helps readers, customers, and internal teams get fast, accurate answers based on your published documentation. When someone clicks **Ask AI**, the assistant searches your docs and returns grounded answers with citations to the relevant pages and sections.

Unlike a generic chatbot, the assistant is:

- **Docs-aware** – it relies on your published documentation as its source of truth.
- **Cited** – it shows where each answer came from in your docs.
- **Configurable** – you can enable it, control how it appears, and tailor how it responds from a dedicated settings area.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The AI Assistant (Ask AI) is separate from the AI Documentation Agent. The **Assistant** answers questions for end users and internal teams. The **Documentation Agent** helps you write and edit content. See [AI Documentation Agent](/ai/ai-documentation-agent) for details.
</Callout>

## How it works

Users open **Ask AI**, enter a question in natural language, and get an answer grounded in your documentation. The assistant can also suggest starter questions to help users begin and follow-up questions to help them continue after each answer.

When the assistant cannot confidently answer from your docs, you can route users to another support channel instead of leaving them at a dead end. You can send them to a support email address or a support URL, depending on how you want your fallback experience to work.

## Citations: how they work and why they matter

Every answer from the assistant can include **citations** back to your documentation.

What users see:

- A short answer and explanation in the chat
- One or more citations linked to specific pages or sections
- The ability to click through to read the full context

Why this is important:

- **Trust** – users can verify information against your canonical docs.
- **Reusability** – support and account teams can safely reuse answers with links in tickets, chats, or emails.
- **Maintainability** – updating your docs improves future answers automatically, and you can trace weak answers back to specific content.

If an answer cannot be strongly supported by your docs, the assistant can ask for clarification or fall back to the support path you configured.

## Customization

Configure the AI Assistant in the dashboard under **Settings → AI Assistant**. You can control whether it appears, how it opens, how it is positioned, and how it guides users through a conversation.

### Availability and display

These settings are in the dashboard under **Settings → AI Assistant → Controls**.

- **Enable AI Assistant** – turn the assistant on or off for your site.
- **Open by Default** – choose whether the assistant starts open when a page loads.
- **Display Mode** – show the assistant as either:
  - **Side Panel**
  - **Floating**
- **Floating position** – when you use Floating mode, position the assistant at the bottom and add extra bottom spacing when needed.

### Entry points

Choose where readers open the assistant. These settings are also under **Settings → AI Assistant → Controls**, in the **Entry Points** block.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-53a37986-2c9e-4094-b9e8-1e1ffae9e9ee/doc-b389b141-ae58-4fd5-91f9-6702fae9ac58/1795774462896-c4dntespvy-ask-ai-entry-points.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2400" height="1516" alt="AI Assistant settings with Enable AI Assistant on and, under Entry points, the Ask AI Button and Ask AI Bar switched on with the bar at page width; beside it, the width menu with Page width and Small." />

- **Ask AI Button** – show the **Ask AI** button in your site's navbar.
- **Ask AI Bar** – show an **Ask a question** bar at the bottom of every page. Readers type a question there, and the assistant opens with that question asked.
- **Ask AI Bar Width** – how wide the bar is:
  - **Page width** – as wide as the page's text.
  - **Small** – a compact bar, centred under the page, that grows a little while a reader types.

Both entry points are on by default, and each can be turned off on its own. They only apply while **Enable AI Assistant** is on: turning the assistant off hides both.

### Conversation behavior

- **Custom Instructions** – add assistant-specific guidance to shape tone, scope, or response behavior. Custom instructions are limited to **500 words**.
- **Starter Questions** – add up to **3** suggested questions to help users begin. Each starter question can be up to **200 characters**.
- **Follow-up Questions** – show **3** suggested follow-up prompts after each answer to help users continue the conversation.

### Fallback and protection

- **Fallback to support** – route users to support when the assistant cannot answer confidently. You can configure fallback to a support email address or a support URL.
- **Invisible CAPTCHA bot protection** – protect the assistant from automated abuse without adding a visible challenge for most users.

### Strict dimensions

Control whether the AI assistant searches across all dimensions or stays scoped to the reader's current context.

- **When enabled** – the assistant only answers from the reader's current product, version, and language, preventing cross-dimension answers that could confuse readers.
- **When disabled or omitted** – the assistant searches across all dimensions and indicates where the answer came from.
- **When to use it** – enable strict mode when your content varies significantly across dimensions and cross-dimension answers may be misleading or incorrect.
- **Reader MCP Server** – the same setting applies when an AI agent filters a search by version, language, or product through the [Reader MCP Server](/ai/reader-mcp-server#versions-languages-and-products).
- **How to enable:**
  - **Via dashboard** – go to Editor → Site Config → Dimensions (Layers icon) → toggle "Strict mode" on.
  - **Via documentation.json** – if you are editing via Code editor, set `search.strict-dimensions` to `true` in `documentation.json`:
    ```json
    {
      "search": {
        "strict-dimensions": true
      }
    }
    ```

  Learn more about how dimensions work in [Dimensions](/organize/dimensions).
</Callout>

## External Sources

The AI Assistant can answer from more than your published documentation. Upload **PDFs** and add **websites** as external knowledge sources, and the assistant searches them alongside your docs and cites them in its answers.

Configure external sources in the dashboard under **AI Assistant** → **Knowledge** → **External Sources**. The content is indexed for retrieval but does not become part of your published documentation or navigation.

See [External Sources](/ai/external-sources) for setup instructions, plan limits, and source settings.

## Where users access the assistant

Readers open the assistant from the entry points you turn on:

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-53a37986-2c9e-4094-b9e8-1e1ffae9e9ee/doc-b389b141-ae58-4fd5-91f9-6702fae9ac58/1795774466882-8aq2vmzcnou-ask-ai-bar.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2400" height="1516" alt="A docs page with the Ask AI button in its navbar and the Ask AI Bar at the bottom with a question typed in; beside it, the assistant answering that question with a citation." />

- **Ask AI Button** – click **Ask AI** in the navbar to open the assistant.
- **Ask AI Bar** – type a question in the **Ask a question** bar at the bottom of the page and press **Enter**. The assistant opens with the question already asked. Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line; the bar grows to fit a longer question.

Then:

1. Readers type a question in natural language or choose a starter question.
2. The assistant responds with:
   - A concise answer or explanation
   - Citations and links to relevant pages or sections
   - Suggested follow-up questions after the answer

How the assistant opens depends on the settings you choose. You can present it as a side panel or as a floating interface, and you can decide whether it opens by default.

### How the Ask AI Bar behaves

- **Stays out of the way of your content** – on longer pages the bar floats at the bottom of the screen and slides away once a reader reaches the end of the page's content, so your page feedback, navigation and footer are never covered. On shorter pages it sits below the page's content.
- **Steps aside while the assistant is open** – the assistant has its own input, so the bar hides until it closes.
- **On phones** – the bar takes the place of the floating **Ask AI** button.
- **Not in embedded docs** – [Embedded Docs](/embedded-docs/overview) have their own Ask AI bar, set up in the embed.

## Use it in your product

The AI Assistant can also appear inside your own product or website, with the same answers and citations as on your docs site. Users can ask from an **Ask AI** bar, a **Help** button, a chat on any screen, or a link beside a setting or an error message.

See [Embedded Docs and AI Assistant](/embedded-docs/overview) to set it up.

## Analytics

Admins can review [Ask AI Analytics](/analytics/overview#ai-assistant) to understand how people use the AI Assistant and where your documentation may need improvement.

Available analytics include:

- **Total questions** – overall question volume
- **Unique sessions** – how many distinct assistant sessions users started
- **Recent questions** – the latest questions asked through the assistant
- **Page context** – where users were in the docs when they asked
- **Export capability** – export analytics data for deeper review and reporting

## Benefits for admins and doc owners

The AI Assistant helps teams do more than answer one-off questions. It can:

- **Help users reach answers faster** – readers can ask natural-language questions instead of hunting across multiple pages.
- **Improve onboarding and adoption** – new users can get quick, cited guidance while learning your product or API.
- **Reduce repetitive support volume** – common documentation questions can be answered directly inside your docs.
- **Support customer-facing teams** – support, success, and sales teams can reuse grounded answers with citations during live conversations.
- **Highlight content gaps** – repeated questions and weak answer coverage show where your docs need clearer explanations or new pages.
