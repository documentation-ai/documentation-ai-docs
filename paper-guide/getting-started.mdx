## Set up your Paper Guide workspace

Set up a Paper Guide workspace from scratch, upload your first papers, and invite team members to collaborate.

<Callout kind="info">

Before you begin, prepare a valid email address, the PDF files you want to upload, and the email addresses of your collaborators.

</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">

  Sign up at [paperguide.io](https://paperguide.io), choose a plan, and verify your email address. Paper Guide offers Free, Team, and Enterprise plans.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a workspace">

  Name your workspace and select a discipline or research category. Configure your default citation style, such as APA, MLA, or Chicago.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload papers">

  Drag and drop your PDF files into the paper library. Paper Guide automatically extracts metadata, including the title, authors, abstract, and DOI. Add tags to organize papers by topic or project.

  <Callout kind="tip">

  You can bulk-upload multiple PDFs at once by dragging them into the library. Metadata extraction runs in the background.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite collaborators">

  Go to **Settings → Members**, enter your collaborators' email addresses, select a role, and send the invitations. Available roles are Owner, Editor, Commenter, and Viewer.

  <Callout kind="tip">

  For quick access without creating accounts, use Share Links from the workspace settings.

  </Callout>

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verification

<Callout kind="info">

Your setup is complete when your workspace shows the uploaded papers with extracted metadata, your collaborators appear in the Members list, and you can find papers using the search bar.

</Callout>
