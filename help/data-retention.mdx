Free workspaces that no one has used for a year are deleted, so we don't keep your data longer than we need to. You get three emails first, and signing in once is enough to keep your workspace.

## Which workspaces are deleted

A workspace is deleted only when all of these are true:

- No member has signed in to or used it for 12 consecutive months. Using an API key, an MCP token, publishing from Git or editing a page all count as use.
- It has never paid for a plan.
- It has no custom domain connected.

Workspaces on a paid plan, workspaces with unused purchased credits, and partner (AppSumo) workspaces are never deleted for inactivity.

## Timeline

<Steps>
<Step title="Three warning emails" icon="mail" title-type="p">
Every member of the workspace gets an email 90, 30 and 7 days before the deletion date. Each email shows the date and what will be deleted.
</Step>
<Step title="Deletion date" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
The workspace is taken offline: its documentation sites stop being served, and its API keys, MCP tokens and embed keys stop working. Members who belong to no other workspace can no longer sign in. Members of other workspaces keep their account.
</Step>
<Step title="21 days to recover" icon="history" title-type="p">
Contact us within 21 days of the deletion date and we can restore the workspace exactly as it was.
</Step>
<Step title="Permanent deletion" icon="trash-2" title-type="p">
After 21 days, the workspace's pages, media, analytics and keys, and the accounts of members with no other workspace, are permanently deleted from our systems and our service providers, within 30 days of the deletion date.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  To keep your workspace, sign in before the deletion date. That's all it takes, and the deletion is cancelled. If you only want a copy of your documentation, use **Download zip** in **Settings > Git** before the deletion date.
</Callout>

## What we keep

- Backups expire on their normal schedule, within 35 days.
- A record that the deletion took place, without names or email addresses, as evidence for audits.
- If you had unsubscribed from our marketing emails, your email address stays on our unsubscribe list so that we never email you again.

For the full details, see section 8 of our [Privacy Policy](https://documentation.ai/legal/privacy) and section 13 of our [Data Processing Addendum](https://documentation.ai/legal/dpa).
