title: Migrate from Docusaurus, Nextra, or Fern

  See what moves when you migrate a Docusaurus, Nextra, or Fern repository to
  Documentation.AI, and which parts of the sidebar you review.
---
You can migrate a Docusaurus, Nextra, or Fern site from its repository. Tell your assistant the folder where you cloned it. The steps are the same as for every platform. See [Migrate to Documentation.AI](/migrations/overview).

To migrate one of these sites from its live address instead, see [Migrate from other sites and Markdown](/migrations/from-markdown).

## Docusaurus

- **Pages** move with their titles, descriptions, headings, text, tables, and code.
- **The generated sidebar** moves as it is: your docs folders, `_category_` files, and `sidebar_position` values.
- **A `sidebars.js` or `sidebars.ts` file** is code, and the migrator does not run code from your repository. Your pages still move, and you review the sidebar at the first decision.
- **Versioned or translated sites** move with every version and language. You review how they are arranged at the first decision.
- **Draft and unlisted pages** do not move, because Docusaurus does not publish them. Your assistant reports how many were left out.

## Nextra

- **Pages** move with their titles, descriptions, headings, text, tables, and code.
- **The sidebar** is written in `_meta` files, which are code the migrator does not run. You review the page order and labels at the first decision.

## Fern

- **The navigation in `fern/docs.yml`** moves as it is, including tabs, versions, and page order.
- **API reference sections** move with their OpenAPI specifications.
- **Files that `docs.yml` does not reference** are not published by Fern, so they are not migrated.

## What needs your decision

- Custom React components, at the second decision
- Any sidebar that comes from code, at the first decision

## What does not move

- Your footer, custom fonts, and banner. See [What does not move](/migrations/overview#what-does-not-move).
