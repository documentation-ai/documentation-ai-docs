## Frequently asked questions

Find answers about using Paper Guide, managing research collaboration, protecting your data, and choosing a plan. Contact support if you need help with a question not covered here.

## Getting started

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What file formats does Paper Guide support?">

Paper Guide supports PDF, LaTeX, and Word document uploads. PDF files receive automatic metadata extraction, including the title, authors, abstract, and DOI. LaTeX files preserve their source formatting and structure.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I import from Zotero or Mendeley?">

Yes. Paper Guide supports direct imports from Zotero and Mendeley libraries through the Integrations page. Connect your account, then select the collections you want to import.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Is there a free plan?">

Yes. The Free plan supports up to 50 papers, 1 GB of storage, and basic search. Team and Enterprise plans offer unlimited papers, advanced search, and additional collaboration features.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Collaboration

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How many collaborators can I invite?">

The Free plan allows up to 3 collaborators per workspace. The Team plan allows up to 25 collaborators. Enterprise plans have no collaborator limit.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can external reviewers access my workspace?">

Yes. Use Share Links to grant view-only or comment access without requiring the reviewer to create a Paper Guide account. You can configure links to expire automatically.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What happens when a collaborator leaves?">

Their edits remain in version history. An Owner or Editor can reassign their papers to another team member and remove their access from Settings → Members.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Data and security

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Is my research data encrypted?">

Yes. Paper Guide encrypts all data at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Enterprise plans support customer-managed encryption keys.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Does Paper Guide train AI models on my papers?">

No. Paper Guide never uses your papers to train AI models. Smart Search runs locally on your indexed data within your workspace.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can I export all my data?">

Yes. Go to Settings → Data Export to download all papers, metadata, and citation data in standard formats, including BibTeX, RIS, and CSV.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Pricing and billing

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I switch plans at any time?">

Yes. Plan changes take effect immediately. Upgrades are prorated based on the remaining billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Do you offer academic discounts?">

Yes. Verified students and academic institutions receive 40% off Team and Enterprise plans. Contact support with your institutional email address to apply.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
